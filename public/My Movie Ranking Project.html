The Best Movies According to Me

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Atlas (2024) – May 24, 2024.

Atlas is a science-fiction action movie about artificial intelligence, humanity, and survival. I like this movie because it explores the relationship between humans and technology while showing how AI can impact our future. Its exciting action scenes and futuristic setting make it an entertaining film.

Treasure Planet (2002) – November 27, 2002.

Treasure Planet is an animated science-fiction adventure about a young boy named Jim Hawkins who discovers a map leading to a legendary hidden treasure. I love this movie because of its beautiful animation, imaginative space adventure, and inspiring story of courage and self-discovery. It reminds me that great adventures begin when we dare to explore the unknown.

The Lion King (1994) – June 24, 1994 (wide theatrical release).

The Lion King is a classic animated film about Simba, a young lion who faces challenges on his journey to becoming king. I love this movie because of its powerful life lessons about family, responsibility, courage, and never giving up. Its memorable characters, beautiful music, and emotional storyline make it timeless. This is my childhood favorite!
