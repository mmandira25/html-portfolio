My Journey and Interest in Computer Technology

I have always been fascinated by computers ever since I took my first computer class during my freshman year of high school. During my college years, I took all the computer-related courses available to me, including a programming language course where I learned Visual Basic Programming language. However, I was unable to take the Web Development course because I had already used up all my college credits.

After graduating from college, I started working as an elementary school teacher teaching Math and Science. However, my passion for computers never faded. I had always wanted to pursue a career in the technology field, but I wasn't sure which path to take: Data Analytics or Web Development.

Over time, I realized that I am more interested in Web Development. After all, it's the internet, where we can create, explore, and do all sorts of amazing things!. I have now decided to pursue a career in Data Analytics, with a stronger focus on Web Development. I hope that taking this course will help me gain new knowledge and skills and bring me one step closer to achieving my goals.
